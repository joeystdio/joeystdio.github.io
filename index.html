About

I’m a Dutch software engineer living in Bangkok since late 2023. I’ve shipped recruitment platforms, telephony systems handling thousands of calls a day, and, since 2018 as lead developer at VBE Group (via Iglu Thailand), worldwide logistics platforms. Mostly .NET and Angular microservices on Google Cloud, TypeScript and Python for the AI work, and a long tail of C++, Java, and everything in between.

The common thread: I like hard problems, I can’t leave a good idea alone, and I ship. Outside work that means an AI note-taker on the App Store, a jukebox that keeps the peace at my friend’s bar, and the developer tools I always wished I had.
