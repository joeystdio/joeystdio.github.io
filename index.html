Personal · Web app

QFD — Queue For Degenerates

Born at a friend's bar, where one mistap on the shared Spotify tablet skips the song someone was waiting for. Instant vibe kill. QFD fixes it: guests scan a QR code and queue songs from their own phones (no app, no account), every pick plays in turn, and nobody can skip anyone else's song. Works for bars, birthdays, or any party. The owner connects Spotify once; everything updates in real time.
